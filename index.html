About the numbers

Values are the theoretical free‑atom form‑factor tables of Chantler (NIST FFAST; J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data 24, 71 (1995); 29, 597 (2000)), extracted with the open‑source xraydb package on the tabulated energy grid and linearly interpolated here. f′ is the anomalous part only (Z not included).

CROSSEC values come from a CCP4 8.0 CROSSEC run (Cromer, J. Appl. Cryst. 16, 437 (1983)) covering 75 elements at 0.55–2.035 Å in 0.015 Å steps (6.09–22.54 keV), linearly interpolated between those points. Not included: H, He, Be, As, Se, Br, Kr and Bi onwards. Compared with Chantler, CROSSEC f″ agrees within about 1%; f′ is lower by up to about 0.6 e⁻ for heavy atoms. The coarse 0.015 Å step means CROSSEC curves are not reliable within a few eV of an edge.
